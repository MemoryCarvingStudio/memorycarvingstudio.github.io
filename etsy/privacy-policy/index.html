Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 9, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Memory Carving Studio Content Publisher handles information when our business uses the app to prepare, approve, publish, and schedule content for its own Pinterest business account.

1. Scope

Memory Carving Studio Content Publisher is a private internal business tool. It is not offered to the public and does not provide services for unrelated Pinterest users. Each Pin is selected and approved by an authorized Memory Carving Studio user before it is published.

2. Information We Access

When an authorized user connects our Pinterest business account, the app may access:

  • basic authenticated account information and account identifiers;
  • the account's own Pins and boards, including the board selected for publication;
  • content and links submitted by our business for publishing; and
  • authorization credentials, such as OAuth access and refresh tokens.

The app does not access other Pinterest users' private data and does not collect sensitive personal information through forms, cookies, or analytics on this website.

3. How We Use Information

We use the information only to:

  • connect the authorized Memory Carving Studio Pinterest business account;
  • display the account's boards so an authorized user can choose a destination;
  • publish or schedule individually approved Pins; and
  • confirm publication status and maintain internal operational records.

We do not use Pinterest data for profiling, advertising, automated engagement, or scraping.

4. Storage and Security

Authorization credentials and operational records are stored on a controlled server within Memory Carving Studio's local network. Access is limited to authorized personnel and is used only for the purposes described in this policy. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, but we use reasonable administrative and technical safeguards appropriate to this internal tool.

5. Sharing and Sale of Data

We do not sell Pinterest data. We do not share Pinterest data with advertisers, data brokers, or unrelated third parties. Information may be disclosed only when required by law or when necessary to protect our legal rights.

6. Retention and Deletion

Authorization credentials are retained while the account remains connected and the app is in use. Operational records are kept only for as long as reasonably necessary for publishing, troubleshooting, security, and legal obligations. When access is revoked or the integration is no longer needed, stored authorization credentials are deleted within a reasonable period.

To request access to or deletion of information associated with this app, email joshua25830268@gmail.com. We may need to verify the request before completing it.

7. Your Choices

An authorized account owner may revoke the app's Pinterest access through Pinterest account settings. Revocation prevents the app from making future Pinterest API requests, although limited records may be retained when legally required.

8. Children's Privacy

This internal business app is not directed to children and is not intended to collect personal information from anyone under the age required by applicable law.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when our practices or legal obligations change. The effective date at the top of this page will identify the latest version.

10. Contact Us

For questions about this Privacy Policy or the app's data practices, contact Memory Carving Studio at joshua25830268@gmail.com.
